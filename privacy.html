Privacy policy

Effective November 9, 2026

ClipOps is a private tool operated by The Place for Life LLC ("we", "us"). It is used only to manage YouTube channels and TikTok accounts that we own. This policy explains what data ClipOps accesses, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Who uses ClipOps

Only us. ClipOps has no public sign-up, and the only accounts connected to it are our own.

Data we access

When we connect one of our accounts using Google's or TikTok's official sign-in screen, ClipOps receives:

  • Account basics: the account's display name, handle, avatar, and ID, used to show which account a clip will post to.
  • Our own videos and their statistics: titles, links, and counts of views, likes, comments, and shares for videos posted from our accounts, used to measure results.
  • Sign-in tokens: access and refresh tokens issued by Google or TikTok, used to post and read statistics on our behalf.

ClipOps does not access other people's accounts, private messages, contacts, followers' personal data, or payment details.

How the data is used

  • To upload clips that a person has reviewed and approved, with the visibility and interaction settings that person chose.
  • To show our own performance data (views and earnings) inside ClipOps.

We do not sell, rent, or share this data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

  • All data is stored on our own computer, not on a cloud server.
  • Sign-in tokens are encrypted with AES-256, using a key protected by the operating system and tied to our user account.
  • ClipOps only accepts connections from the computer it runs on and requires a password plus a one-time code to open.

Retention and deletion

Performance data is kept while an account is connected. Disconnecting an account in ClipOps deletes its stored tokens immediately. Clip video files are deleted from our computer after they are posted.

YouTube API Services

ClipOps uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel to ClipOps, the account owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.

TikTok

ClipOps uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API to post to our own TikTok accounts and read their video statistics. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions. TikTok's handling of data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Instagram and Facebook

ClipOps uses Meta's Instagram API with Facebook Login to post Reels to our own Instagram professional accounts and read their view counts. It receives the Facebook Pages linked to those accounts and an access token for each, which are encrypted and stored on our computer. Access can be removed at any time in Facebook under Settings and privacy, Settings, Business integrations, or in Instagram under Settings, Apps and websites. Meta's handling of data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
