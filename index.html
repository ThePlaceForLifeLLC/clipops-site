A private clip scheduler for our own channels

ClipOps is a private tool run by The Place for Life LLC. It helps us prepare short vertical clips for creators who have hired us to clip their content, then posts those clips to YouTube and TikTok accounts that we own and operate.

How it works

  1. We connect our own YouTube channels and TikTok accounts with the official sign-in screens from Google and TikTok.
  2. ClipOps prepares clips from source videos that creators provide for their clipping campaigns.
  3. A person reviews every clip, chooses who can see it, and sets its comment, duet, stitch, and paid-partnership options before it posts.
  4. ClipOps reads view counts for our own posts so we can report results to the creators we work with.

ClipOps is not a public service. It has no sign-ups and is not offered to other users. It runs on our own computer, and the data it handles stays there.
